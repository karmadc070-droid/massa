Safety

So that opening
the door is fine

You are letting a stranger into your home. Reducing that weight is where most of our work has gone.

01

Three-stage vetting

Only therapists who clear a credential check, an identity check and an in-person interview get the verified badge.

02

Hygiene kit check

Partners confirmed to use single-use consumables carry a separate mark on their profile.

03

Safety button

If something goes wrong mid-service, one tap sends your location straight to our team.

Inappropriate requests are barred on both sides,
and confirmed breaches end access immediately.
