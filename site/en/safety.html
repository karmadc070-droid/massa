Safety & vetting

You are letting a
stranger into your home

That is the hardest part of this service, and the part we have worked on most.

01

Credentials

We check massage and beauty qualifications and work history. Paperwork alone is not enough.

02

Identity

Government ID confirms real name and age. Documents sit in private storage that only reviewers can open.

03

In-person interview

We meet each applicant and assess manner and communication. All three must pass before the badge appears.

Hygiene

Single-use by default

For anything touching skin directly — waxing, nails — hygiene is the whole job. Wax, strips and files are single-use and never reused.

Partners we have confirmed on this carry a hygiene mark on their profile. Its absence does not mean poor hygiene, but it lets you choose the ones we have checked.

During the service

If something goes wrong

01

Safety button

The in-app SOS sends your current location to our team immediately.

02

Location sharing

You can share your location with someone you trust for the duration of the service.

03

Report and block

Reports are reviewed, acted on and answered. Blocking stops that person being matched to you again.

Not allowed

The same rule
on both sides

Inappropriate requests, sexual ones included, are barred for customers and providers alike. A confirmed breach ends access immediately and may be reported to the authorities depending on severity.

Cash deals outside the app and requests for personal contact details are also barred. Refuse them and report. A transaction that never went through the app leaves no record, so we cannot help if it goes wrong.

Limits

What we cannot guarantee

Plainly: massa is a platform, and we cannot fully control what people do.

Vetting lowers the odds of something going wrong. It does not take them to zero. The care offered is for relaxation and upkeep and is not a medical treatment — please see a medical professional for anything that needs treating.

What we will do, when something does go wrong, is check the records, establish the facts and take the steps that follow.
