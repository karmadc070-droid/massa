Straight talk

You are suspicious.
You should be.

Most massage therapist ads online are scams or something else entirely. So here is only what you can check for yourself.

01

We ask for documents

Both sides of your ID are required. We review every application by hand before approving. Scammers never ask for paperwork.

02

We take nothing from you

No joining fee, no training fee, no deposit, no monthly fee. If anyone asks you to pay first, that is not us.

03

The app and the company are real

massa is on the App Store and Google Play. Download it and look before you decide.

04

We write what is true

Unchecked documents get no verification mark, and we say so to customers. We do not inflate anyone.

What we expect

The commitment

A booking you accept is a booking you finish. Repeated declines lower your visibility, and enough of them pause your ability to take bookings for a while.

Single-use hygiene kits are the standard. Making inappropriate requests of a customer, or taking a customer off the app, means immediate suspension.

It runs both ways. If a customer makes an inappropriate request, refuse and report it. The app has a safety button — one tap sends your location to our team. The standard applies to both sides.
